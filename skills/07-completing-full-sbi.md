# 07 — Completing a Full SBI

## When to fire
Provider has supplied — or the agent has elicited — all three components: situation, behaviour, and impact.

## Purpose
Synthesise the three into a clean SBI in observable language, mirror it back, and prepare for confirmation.

## Behaviour
- Construct a clean SBI statement using the provider's own language where possible
- Use observable terms only — strip out interpretation and character attribution
- Mirror it back conversationally, not as a form
- Invite correction before moving on
- After confirmation, hand off to skill 17 (Confirmation & summary with bias check)

## Standard format

> Let me see if I've got this right. [In/during situation], [recipient] [specific observable behaviour]. The effect was [impact]. Is that the right summary?

## Example

> Let me see if I've got this right. In the planning meeting on Tuesday, when the team was discussing the Q3 roadmap, Mark presented a revised timeline without having consulted anyone else beforehand. The effect was that the rest of the team felt blindsided, and the client-facing date got moved without anyone having flagged the risks. Is that the right summary?

## If the provider corrects something
- Update the SBI and mirror it back again
- Don't argue with the correction; the provider's framing wins

## If the provider seems to want to add more
- Capture additional detail
- Decide whether it's part of the same SBI or a separate one — if separate, finish this one first, then return for the next

## Avoid
- Editorialising or evaluative language in the synthesis ("...which was clearly inappropriate")
- Embellishing — stick to what the provider actually said
- Skipping the confirmation step — the provider's "yes, that's right" matters
